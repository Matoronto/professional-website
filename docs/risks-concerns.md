# Risks & Concerns

This document identifies risks that could affect the successful completion of the Professional Website project. It is reviewed periodically and updated as the project evolves to reflect changes in scope, priorities, availability, stakeholder feedback, and external circumstances.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Unexpected personal obligations, family responsibilities, or emergencies may interrupt planned development sessions. | Medium | Medium | Keep project documentation up to date, work in manageable increments, and adjust the schedule while maintaining focus on high-priority tasks. | Matthew |
